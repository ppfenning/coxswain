# 0.4.0

Cut on 2026-09-08. Every component is tagged `v0.4.0` in lockstep; `coxswain-tools` is published to
PyPI from that tag. Status: **beta**. **Breaking:** `cox route leader` is now `cox route chair`; anything
scripted against the old names must change before upgrading.

## coxswain-tools

- `cox route leader take|beat|release|status` → `cox route chair …`; `agent_tools/leader.py` stays as a
  deprecation shim re-exporting from `agent_tools.chair`, and its docstring names when it may go (tools #94,
  #96, #98, #99, #100, #102).
- Historical run logs stay readable: `events.py` parses both `leader taken:` and `chair taken:`
  permanently, with a test on an old-worded line (tools #92, #95, #101).
- `cox route chair clear` removes a lock whose holder is provably not live, refuses a live one without
  `--force`, and holds the lock across read-decide-unlink (tools #99).
- `cox stats ingest` (preview): a SQLite schema (tools #93), pure derivations (tools #97), the ingest CLI (tools #103),
  and trace recovery for budget-stopped runs that write no `usage.json` — 101 calls worth `$29.20` on the
  development corpus (tools #104). No query surface yet. The stats-store contract and spike verdict are in the
  repository (tools #91).
- `pyproject` bumped to match the tag (tools #105).

## coxswain-cartridges

- `plan` gets a measured `$0.60` ceiling: over 310 calls, p95 `$0.317` and a maximum of `$0.345` against
  `$0.35` — a distribution that stops `$0.005` short of its limit is censored (cartridges #54).
- `decompose` gets its own `$0.60` ceiling; it had inherited a number calibrated from plan nodes, and
  three identical runs cost `$0.45`, `$0.39` and `$0.22` — the problem is variance (cartridges #52, #53).

## coxswain

`releasable.yml`: a nightly, manually dispatchable check that the umbrella and all four components are green on their default branches (#33). Manifest and notes (#34, #35).

## Known gaps

`cox stats` has `ingest` and nothing else, and the calls-to-task attribution is not applied. `releasable.yml` shipped as invalid YAML and never ran; found and fixed in `0.5.0`.

## Components

`coxswain-tools` carries the rename, `chair clear` and the stats preview; `coxswain-cartridges` the
ceilings; `coxswain` the workflow. `coxswain-graphs` and `coxswain-crew` are unchanged since `v0.3.0`.
