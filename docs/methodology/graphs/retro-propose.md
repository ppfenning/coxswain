# retro-propose

The coxswain dispatches retro-propose once an epic closes: it reads the
run records for every ticket in the epic and drafts what went well, what
slowed the epic down, and what to change. What lands is a draft retro for
a human to accept or edit, never a retro posted on the human's behalf.
